HOW A SCENE MOVES THROUGH THE SYSTEM

01

Denoise

An adaptive Lee filter removes SAR speckle without blurring slick boundaries.

02

Segment

Every pixel is classified as sea or oil spill.

03

Classify confidence

Per-class precision, recall, F1, and IoU are computed against the label.

04

Verdict

The segmentation mask is overlaid onto the original scene for responser triage.
